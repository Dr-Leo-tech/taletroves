About TaleTrove

TaleTrove is a story-sharing platform where authors publish original fiction chapter by chapter and readers discover, like, and comment on what they read. It was created by an independent developer in Nigeria who wanted a simple, welcoming place for new writers to be read, and for readers to find stories they would not meet anywhere else.

Why TaleTrove exists

Many people write stories but never share them, often because they do not know where to start or fear nobody will read their work. At the same time, readers are looking for fresh voices beyond the usual bestseller lists. TaleTrove connects the two. Writers get a clean space to publish and an audience that can respond to each chapter. Readers get a growing library of original tales, with direct feedback channels to the people who wrote them.

How it works for readers

  • Browse freely. Visit the Stories page or use guest mode to read without signing up.
  • Read chapter by chapter. Every story has a table of contents, so you can move through at your own pace and return to any chapter.
  • Join the conversation. Create a free account to like stories and leave comments. Encouraging, thoughtful feedback is what keeps authors writing.
  • Find more from authors you enjoy. Each author has a profile page that lists everything they have published.

How it works for authors

  • Register as an author when you sign up. It is free.
  • Publish a story by giving it a title and opening description, then add chapters as you finish them. You do not need to complete a whole book before sharing.
  • Hear from readers. Likes and comments on your stories appear so you can see what connects with your audience.
  • Keep ownership. Your stories remain yours. We only ask for the permission needed to display them on TaleTrove. See our Terms of Service for the details.

Community guidelines

TaleTrove works because people treat each other with respect. We ask everyone to follow a few simple principles:

  • Publish only your own original work, or work you have permission to share. Plagiarism and copyright infringement are not allowed.
  • Give feedback that is honest but kind. Harassment, hate speech, and personal attacks have no place here.
  • Do not post content that is illegal, sexually explicit involving minors, or promotes violence against others.
  • Do not spam, impersonate other people, or try to disrupt the service.

We may remove content or suspend accounts that break these rules. If you see something that should not be on TaleTrove, please tell us through the Contact page and we will look into it.

Your privacy

We collect only what we need to run the platform, we never sell personal information, and we explain exactly how data is handled in our Privacy Policy. Your email address is never shown to other users.

What is coming next

TaleTrove is growing. We are working on an Android app, better tools for authors, and an optional coin system that will let readers support the writers they love and unlock premium chapters. We will always keep a generous amount of free reading available, and any change that affects how your data is used will be reflected in our policies first.

Get in touch

Have a question, an idea, or a bug to report? We would love to hear from you. Visit our Contact page to send a message. If you are a writer, create an author account and share your first chapter today.
