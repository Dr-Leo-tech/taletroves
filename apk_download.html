About this app

Read. Write. Share your stories.

TaleTrove is a free story-sharing community for writers and readers. Authors publish their fiction chapter by chapter, and readers discover new worlds, follow the tales they love, and talk about them with others.

Why people choose TaleTrove

  • • Discover new stories: Browse the latest tales from authors in the community.
  • • Comfortable reading: Read chapter by chapter in a clean layout built for your phone.
  • • Write and publish: Register as an author, start a new story, and post chapters from your dashboard.
  • • Like and comment: Show love for your favourite stories and join the conversation.
  • • Author profiles: See an author's stories, total likes and comments in one place.
  • • Browse as a guest: Start reading right away, and sign up when you're ready to join in.
  • • Always up to date: New stories and improvements show up automatically, with no reinstalling.

How to install

  1. Tap Download APK and wait for it to finish.
  2. Open the downloaded file.
  3. If Android blocks it, tap Settings and allow installs from this browser.
  4. Go back and tap Install, then open TaleTrove.
