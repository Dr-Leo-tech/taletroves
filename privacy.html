Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

TaleTrove is a story-sharing platform where authors publish stories chapter by chapter and readers discover, like, and comment on them. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

TaleTrove is run by an independent developer based in Nigeria. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean TaleTrove. You can reach us any time through our Contact page.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address, username, and password when you sign up. Your password is handled by our authentication provider and is stored in hashed form, so we never see it.
  • Content you create: stories, chapters, comments, likes, and any details you add to your profile.
  • Messages you send us: the name, email, subject, and message you enter in the contact form.
  • Usage information: we use Google Analytics to see which pages are visited and how. This can include your device and browser type, approximate location, and the pages you view.
  • Browser storage: a login session is kept in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in between visits.

3. How we use your information

  • To create your account and let you read, write, like, and comment on stories.
  • To show your username and published content to other users.
  • To reply to messages you send us.
  • To understand how TaleTrove is used so we can fix problems and improve it.
  • To keep the platform safe and enforce our Terms.

4. What other people can see

Your username, your published stories and chapters, your comments, and like counts are public. Your email address is never shown to other users.

5. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We use a few services to run TaleTrove, and they process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase: database and user authentication.
  • Netlify: website hosting.
  • Google Analytics: usage statistics.
  • Paystack: planned payment provider for coin purchases. When payments launch, card and bank details will be handled by Paystack and not stored by us.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users and platform.

6. How long we keep your information

We keep your account information and content for as long as your account is active. Contact-form messages are kept for as long as needed to deal with your request. If you ask us to delete your account, we will remove your personal information and your content, except where we are required by law to keep something.

7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop using it in certain ways. We handle these requests in line with applicable Nigerian data protection law, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. To make a request, use our Contact page. You can also opt out of Google Analytics with a browser add-on or your browser's privacy settings.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and access rules on our database. No online service can be completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children

TaleTrove is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you are under 18, please use TaleTrove with the permission of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Where your data is processed

Our service providers may store and process data on servers outside Nigeria. By using TaleTrove you understand your information may be transferred to those locations.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as TaleTrove grows, for example when coin payments launch. When we make a meaningful change, we will update the date at the top of this page.

12. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Reach us through the Contact page.
