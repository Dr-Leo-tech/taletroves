Welcome to TaleTrove

TaleTrove is a free story-sharing community built for writers and readers. Authors publish their fiction chapter by chapter, and readers discover new worlds, follow the tales they love, and talk about them with the people who wrote them. Whether you are drafting your first short story or looking for your next favourite series, TaleTrove gives you a simple, friendly place to do it.

For readers

Browse the latest stories without even creating an account by using guest mode. When you find something you enjoy, sign up to like stories, leave comments, and keep track of what you are reading. Every story is split into chapters, so you can read at your own pace and pick up where you left off.

For authors

Register as an author to publish your own stories, add chapters as you write them, and hear directly from your readers. Your author profile collects all of your work in one place, so new readers can find everything you have written.
